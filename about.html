Who we are

A shared purpose.
A practical approach.

Frantomapa Educational Aid is a non-profit charity organisation formed in Ghana on 8 May 2016. Based in Accra, we bring together concerned educationists from Ghana and the United Kingdom.

We work to expand access to quality education and nurture the skills young people need to contribute to Ghana’s human and technological development.

Our work supports Sustainable Development Goals 4 and 5: quality education and gender equality.

What we do

Support that makes learning possible.

  1. 01

    Learning resources

    Educational materials and facilities for less endowed schools and communities.

  2. 02

    Educational access

    Support for brilliant but disadvantaged children to access quality education.

  3. 03

    STEM in practice

    Science, technology, engineering and mathematics quiz competitions and exhibitions.

  4. 04

    Guidance & mentorship

    Counselling and mentorship to help young people find their direction.

  5. 05

    Knowledge to share

    Publishing educational materials that support learning.

Why it matters

From learning to solving problems.

Frantomapa grew from concern about pupils’ performance in mathematics, science and technology, and the need for more opportunities to put learning into practice.

We believe STEM belongs in everyday life. Our goal is to help children use what they learn to tackle challenges in their communities and beyond.
