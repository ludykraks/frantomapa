Who we are

Every child deserves the opportunity to keep learning.

Frantomapa Educational Aid is a non-governmental organisation formed in Ghana on 8 May 2016. From our base in Accra, we work to expand educational opportunity and strengthen STEM learning.

Our work

Turning support into practical change.

Our projects focus on learning environments, teachers and access to technology.

Technology • 2021

Rural Technology Centre

We established a Technology Centre in rural Ghana to bring our STEM-focused mission closer to children and communities.

Teachers • 2019

Classroom Management Workshop

Training for teachers at Belcoff School Complex in classroom management, learning resources and jolly phonics.

Infrastructure • 2017

A Safer Classroom

After finding children learning in a dangerous building at Tinkong Presbyterian KG, we raised funds for a new classroom block.
